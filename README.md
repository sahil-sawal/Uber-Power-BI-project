# 🚖 Uber Trip Analysis Dashboard
## 📌 Project Title / Headline

Uber Trip Analysis – Interactive Power BI Dashboard for Ride Data Insights

## 📝 Short Description / Purpose

This project provides a comprehensive data analysis of Uber rides through an interactive Power BI dashboard. The primary purpose is to explore booking patterns, customer preferences, revenue generation, and operational performance. By analyzing trip-level details such as payment methods, vehicle types, trip distances, and timings, the dashboard enables data-driven decision-making for ride-hailing operations.

## ▫️It is designed for:

- Business stakeholders to track revenue and demand.

- Operations managers to optimize fleet allocation.

- Data analysts to explore patterns across time, location, and customer behavior.

- Researchers interested in mobility, urban transport, and ride-hailing analytics.

## 📂 Data Source

The analysis is based on Uber trip records for June 2024, containing detailed information such as:

- Trip ID

- Pickup date & hour

- Vehicle type (UberX, Uber Comfort, Uber Black, Uber Green, UberXL)

- Payment method (Uber Pay, Cash, Amazon Pay, Google Pay)

- Number of passengers

- Trip distance (miles)

- Booking value (USD)

- Pickup & drop-off locations

- The raw data was processed, cleaned, and transformed into a structured dataset for visualization in Power BI.

## ✨ Features / Highlights
### 📊 High-Level KPIs

- 103.7K Total Bookings within the analysis period.

- $1.6M Total Booking Value, with an average of $15 per ride.

- 349K Total Trip Miles, average of 3 miles per trip.

- 16 minutes Average Trip Time across all bookings.

### 💳 Payment Insights

- Majority of trips are paid via Uber Pay (67%), followed by Cash (32%).

- Minimal adoption of Amazon Pay and Google Pay.


### 🌙 Day vs Night Analysis

- Daytime trips (62.55%) dominate over Nighttime trips (37.45%), showing higher ride demand during working hours and evening commutes.


### 🚘 Vehicle Type Analysis

- UberX leads with 38K+ trips, generating $5.83M revenue and covering the longest distance.

- Premium services like Uber Black, Uber Comfort, and UberXL cater to smaller yet high-value segments.

- Uber Green provides an eco-friendly option, with over 14K bookings.


### 📍 Location Analysis

- Most frequent pickup: Penn Station / Madison Sq West.

- Most frequent drop-off: Upper East Side North.

- Longest trip: 144.1 miles (Lower East Side → Crown Heights North).


### ⏱ Time Analysis

- Peak hours: 12 PM – 6 PM, when demand is highest.

- Low activity: 12 AM – 4 AM, indicating minimal night bookings.

- Weekday vs Weekend Trends:

- Weekdays show steady demand, with Wednesday being the busiest.

- Saturdays (18.7K bookings) and Sundays (19.2K bookings) record the highest demand.


### 🔍 Detailed Trip Logs

The dashboard includes a detailed view of each trip, allowing filtering and analysis by:

- Date & time of ride.

- Vehicle type and payment mode.

- Passenger count.

- Booking value and distance.

- Pickup location.


### 🚀 Why This Dashboard Matters

- Provides clear visibility of revenue streams and customer behavior.

- Helps optimize driver allocation based on demand hotspots and peak times.

- Supports strategic planning by identifying high-value trips and customer preferences.
  
- Offers granular insights from daily patterns to individual ride details.

- This project demonstrates the power of business intelligence (BI) tools in turning raw transportation data into meaningful insights that can drive efficiency, increase revenue, and improve customer experience.
